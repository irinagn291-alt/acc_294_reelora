Version 1.0 is the first release of Periplus, a one-book reading log.

• Introduction with Skip, Next, and Continue
• Map with True bearing, Curl, Log, and Set finish day
• Catalogue with Search a title, Open Library, Scan ISBN, and Enter a book
• ISBN finder, ISBN digits, and Simulator samples
• Voyage with Finish day, Open voyage, and Finish the book
• Stats for Landfalls, Legs, Set marks, Buoys, and Recalibrations
• Settings with Replay introduction, Delete the route, and Contact

Portrait only, on iPhone and iPad. Dark appearance only. Minimum iOS 17.0.
