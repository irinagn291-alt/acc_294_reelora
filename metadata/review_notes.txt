Thanks for reviewing this submission. We retested the build under review on a clean device. We could not reproduce a crash, a dead end, an empty screen or a non-functioning feature.

Three behaviours are deliberate and can look unfinished on a short pass. Please try these first.

1. Rows under On this device on Catalogue are not buttons and do not open a voyage. After Save book, open Voyage, pick a Volume, set Finish day to a later day than today, then tap Open voyage.
2. Search a title left blank never queries Open Library. Type a title, or use Scan ISBN or Enter a book. A blank field only filters books already on the device.
3. Open voyage success shows The expedition is open from today. and leaves the sheet open. Tap Log pages or swipe down to return to the map.

Full review path. No account, no login:
- Cold launch is a dark system launch screen with no wordmark. On a physical device: three introduction pages, Skip at the top trailing corner. 1. A route for one volume. Next. 2. Log the pages you read today. Next. 3. Set when the curl reaches its cap. Continue. Skip and Continue both open the map. No name, account, or permission is asked.
- Map stays on screen. Toolbar: Catalogue, Voyage, Stats, Settings. Sheets swipe down to close. Empty: No voyage yet. Open a book. That control opens Catalogue.
- Empty Catalogue: The shelf is empty. Search a title, Scan ISBN, or Enter a book. Save book is dim until Title is filled and Total pages is at least 1. ISBN: Point the camera at the ISBN barcode, or type the digits. Continue is the only control that leads to the system camera alert. Use these digits stays dim until 8 digits. Simulator only: Simulator samples.
- Voyage: empty shelf, Open the catalogue. Else pick Volume, Finish day (twenty-one days from now; not today or earlier), Open voyage, then Log pages.
- Map underway: Today's pages defaults to 1. Fewer pages, More pages, Log 1 pages. Idle days curl the line off True bearing. At forty-five degrees, Log is replaced by The curl has reached forty five degrees. Set the finish day before the next log. and Set finish day. Voyage offers Finish the book for remaining pages.
- Stats empty: No landfalls yet. Then Landfalls, Legs, Set marks, Buoys, Recalibrations.
- Settings: Replay introduction (Skip or Continue returns to the map), Delete the route, Contact (spoken Contact Periplus) opens the support page.

On the Simulator only, the first run can open already underway on Northwater Atlas by I. Marlow, 200 pages, ISBN 9780000000002, with four legs so Log is enabled. A physical device has no starter book. Unfinished work resumes on relaunch with the same shelf, voyage, legs, buoys, curl, and finish day. Today's pages starts at 1 again.

Technical facts: no accounts, no in-app purchases, no ads, no analytics, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Camera is requested only after Continue on the ISBN sheet if the finder is used instead of typing. Usage string: Periplus uses the camera to read the ISBN barcode printed on a book so that volume can be added to the catalogue on this device. No other permission is asked. Books, voyages, legs, buoys, set marks, and landfalls stay on this device. Lookup can use Open Library; a saved book remains available when the network does not. English interface. Dates and page counts follow the device locale and calendar. No region switch and no country lock. Portrait only, iPhone and iPad, dark appearance only, iOS 17.0 and later.
